# Section 1 Simulator only
This handbook describes the virtual vehicle of the lab driving simulator. It is not valid for any real vehicle and must never be used on public roads.

# Section 2 Warning lights
An amber tyre symbol means the simulated tyre pressure is low: reduce speed and stop at the next simulated service area. A red temperature symbol means the simulated engine is overheating: stop safely and switch off the simulated engine.

# Section 3 Lane keeping assist
Lane keeping assist is switched on with the steering wheel button marked LKA. It works above 60 km/h and only on roads with visible lane markings.

# Section 4 Ending a session
To end a session, bring the vehicle to a full stop, engage the parking brake and press the red END button on the operator console.
