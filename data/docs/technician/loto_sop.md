# Section 1 Purpose
This procedure prevents unexpected start-up or release of stored energy during maintenance. It applies to every machine in the training workshop.

# Section 2 Lockout steps
Inform the operator and the shift supervisor that you will lock out the machine. Switch the machine off at the local control. Open the main isolator in the electrical cabinet and apply your personal red padlock and a signed tag. Each person working on the machine applies their own lock. Release or block stored energy: bleed hydraulic and pneumatic pressure, and drain liquid lines.

# Section 3 Verify zero energy
Try to start the machine with the normal start control; it must not start. Measure the absence of voltage at the motor terminals with an approved voltage tester. Return the start control to off after the test.

# Section 4 Removing locks
Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. Before removing your lock, make sure all tools are removed, guards are refitted and all people are clear of the machine. If the owner of a lock is not available, only the shift supervisor may remove it, following the lost-key procedure.

# Section 5 Lost key procedure
If a personal lock key is lost, report it to the shift supervisor at once. The supervisor confirms that the lock owner has left the site before cutting the lock.
