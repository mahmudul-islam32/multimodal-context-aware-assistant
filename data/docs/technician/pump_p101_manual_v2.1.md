# Section 1 Scope
This manual covers routine maintenance of the centrifugal pump P-101 (end-suction, 7.5 kW motor) used in the training workshop. Only trained technicians may work on the pump.

# Section 2 Safety before any work
Isolate the pump electrically and apply your personal lock according to the lockout tagout procedure LOTO-01 before opening any cover. Close the suction valve V-11 and the discharge valve V-12 and attach a tag to each valve. Open the drain valve V-13 and let the casing drain completely into the drip tray. Verify zero energy by pressing the local start button: the motor must not turn. Wear safety glasses and cut-resistant gloves during all steps.

# Section 3 Tools and parts
You need a 13 mm and a 17 mm socket, a torque wrench covering 10 to 60 Nm, a seal puller, lint-free cloths and the seal kit SK-101-B. Use only the grease supplied with the seal kit.

# Section 4.1 Removing the coupling guard
Remove the four M8 screws of the coupling guard with the 13 mm socket and lift the guard off. Store the screws in the parts tray.

# Section 4.2 Removing the old mechanical seal
Loosen the gland follower bolts evenly, half a turn at a time. Pull the seal cartridge out with the seal puller. Inspect the shaft sleeve for scoring; replace the sleeve if the grooves are deeper than 0.2 mm.

# Section 4.3 Seal replacement
Clean the seal seat with a lint-free cloth. Apply a thin film of the supplied grease to the O-rings of the new seal. Slide the new seal cartridge onto the shaft without tilting it. Tighten the gland follower bolts to 25 Nm in a cross pattern. Do not exceed 25 Nm, because over-tightening cracks the seal face.

# Section 4.4 Refitting and alignment
Refit the coupling guard and tighten its M8 screws to 12 Nm. Check the coupling alignment with a straight edge; the maximum allowed offset is 0.1 mm.

# Section 5 Restart and leak test
Close the drain valve V-13. Open the suction valve V-11 fully, then open the discharge valve V-12 slowly. Remove your lock and tags only after all tools are cleared from the pump. Start the pump and run it for 10 minutes. A new seal may drip up to 5 drops per minute during the first 10 minutes; after that, no leakage is allowed. If leakage continues, stop the pump and report to the shift supervisor.

# Section 6 Bearing lubrication
Grease the motor bearings every 2000 operating hours with 15 g of lithium grease per bearing. Do not over-grease; excess grease raises bearing temperature.

# Section 7 Troubleshooting
If the pump vibrates strongly, stop it and check the coupling alignment. If the motor trips on overload, check that the discharge valve V-12 is not fully open against an empty system. Noise like gravel in the casing indicates cavitation: check that the suction valve V-11 is fully open and the suction strainer is clean.
