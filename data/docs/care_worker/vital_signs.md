# Section 1 Routine observations
Measure blood pressure, pulse, respiratory rate, temperature and oxygen saturation once per day for stable residents, and every 4 hours for residents flagged as unwell.

# Section 2 Escalation thresholds
Call the nurse in charge immediately if the respiratory rate is above 24 per minute, if oxygen saturation is below 92 percent, if systolic blood pressure is below 90 mmHg, or if the resident is newly confused. Do not wait for the next round.

# Section 3 Measuring respiratory rate
Count breaths for a full 60 seconds while the resident is resting and unaware, for example while you still hold the wrist after taking the pulse.
