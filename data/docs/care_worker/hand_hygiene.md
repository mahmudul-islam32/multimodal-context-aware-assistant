# Section 1 Five moments
Clean your hands before touching a resident, before a clean or aseptic task, after a risk of body fluid exposure, after touching a resident, and after touching the resident's surroundings.

# Section 2 Hand rub technique
Use alcohol-based hand rub when hands are not visibly soiled. Apply 3 ml of hand rub into a cupped hand and rub all surfaces of both hands for 30 seconds until dry.

# Section 3 Hand washing
Wash hands with soap and water when they are visibly soiled, and after caring for a resident with diarrhoea or suspected norovirus, because alcohol rub is not reliable against norovirus. Wash for 40 to 60 seconds and dry with a single-use towel.

# Section 4 Gloves
Gloves do not replace hand hygiene. Clean your hands before putting on gloves and immediately after removing them. Change gloves between residents and between dirty and clean tasks on the same resident.
