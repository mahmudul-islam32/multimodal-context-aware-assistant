# Section 1 Frequency
Reposition residents who cannot move independently at least every 2 hours during the day and every 4 hours at night if they lie on a pressure-redistributing mattress. Record each position change on the turning chart.

# Section 2 Technique
Always use two carers and a slide sheet for residents who cannot help with the move. Raise the bed to working height before you start and lower it again afterwards. Never drag the resident across the sheet, because friction damages the skin.

# Section 3 30 degree tilt
Use the 30 degree lateral tilt: place a pillow behind the back and between the knees so that the sacrum and the hip bone are free of pressure. Keep the head of the bed at or below 30 degrees unless the care plan says otherwise.

# Section 4 Skin check
At each repositioning, check the sacrum, heels, hips and elbows for redness. Press a red area lightly: if the redness does not fade, report it to the nurse in charge the same shift.
